Why this website exists

The problem

Travel information about Uganda is scattered across many sites, and visitors struggle to know which guides, lodges and permits they can trust.

Who it is for

International tourists, Ugandans exploring home, students on study trips and families planning holidays on any budget.

Our solution

One trusted platform to discover destinations, compare experiences and book trips with licensed local guides.

Our story

Born from a love of Uganda

ExploreUganda is a tourism platform dedicated to showcasing the natural beauty, rich culture and unique experiences that make Uganda the Pearl of Africa. We believe travel creates understanding, supports local communities and leaves places better than we found them.

Meet the team

Our Mission

To promote Uganda's top tourist attractions and create memorable travel experiences for visitors from around the world.

Our Vision

To be the leading platform for Uganda tourism, inspiring global travellers to explore, experience and support sustainable tourism.
